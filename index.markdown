# Matheus Fernandes Moreno

![me](/assets/me.jpg){: style="float: right; width: 25%; margin-left: 5%;"}

I'm a second-year master's student in Computer Science, on the Quantum
Information track, at [Sorbonne University][su]. I'm currently carrying out my
capstone internship at the [National Institute of Informatics][nii].

I obtained my undergraduate degree in Electronic and Computer Engineering from
the [Federal University of Rio de Janeiro][ufrj]. Before starting my graduate
studies, I worked as a machine learning engineer for three years.

I am interested in myriad research topics, from quantum information processing
to the foundational aspects of quantum mechanics. My present work involves
estimation and distillation protocols for quantum networks.

You can reach me at `matheus [dot] fernandes_moreno [at] etu.sorbonne-universite.fr`.
